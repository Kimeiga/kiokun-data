# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Kiokun serves language learners, polyglot readers, and language-tool builders who need dense Chinese, Japanese, and Korean dictionary data without losing the links between scripts, readings, examples, components, and meanings. The blog is for technically curious users and peers who want to understand how the data and model choices behind the product are made.

## Product Purpose

Kiokun is a multilingual dictionary and learning system that combines Chinese, Japanese, and Korean data into searchable, learner-facing records. Success means users can inspect a word or character from several language angles, trust the examples and explanations, and see enough provenance to understand why the app behaves the way it does.

## Brand Personality

Careful, technical, transparent. Kiokun should feel like a serious learning tool built by someone willing to show the machinery, not a generic language-learning wrapper.

## Anti-references

Avoid template SaaS gloss, vague AI claims, cute gamified clutter, and model-benchmark pages that hide the raw outputs. Public research pages should not look like dashboards pasted into marketing copy; they should read as clear engineering notes with the data close at hand.

## Design Principles

- Show the evidence near the claim.
- Preserve linguistic precision over decorative simplification.
- Keep dense data scannable without flattening nuance.
- Make model and data tradeoffs explicit.
- Prefer useful artifacts people can download, inspect, and reproduce.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-readable article structure, reduced-motion fallbacks, and CJK font stacks that preserve legibility across Chinese, Japanese, and Korean content.
